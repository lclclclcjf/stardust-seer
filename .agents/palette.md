# Palette Notes

- The draw flow separates shuffling from selection: no draw is persisted until the asker has chosen the required cards and confirms the set.
- Card selection is reversible until confirmation, ten-card spreads offer manual or animated automatic selection, and card backs are always inherited from the chosen theme and concrete AI deck.
- Reveal order is user-controlled by directly clicking any card back; after all cards are visible, navigation to the reading remains an explicit user action.
